# Vision

petnudge is a pixel pet that lives on your desktop and nudges you: your
todos, your pomodoro, and your coding agent. These are the principles
decisions are checked against. When a feature fails one of them, it is not
added.

1. **The pet comes to you.** You should not have to open anything to know
   what is next. The pet says it, briefly, at the right moment, and then
   gets out of the way.
2. **Calm, not nagging.** One bubble at a time, short words, easy to snooze.
   Music, chat and sounds are off until you turn them on.
3. **Yours, on your machine.** Data is a local SQLite file. No account, no
   server, no telemetry. AI features use your own login or key, and only
   when you ask.
4. **Agents are first-class users.** Anything you can do in the panel, an
   agent can do through MCP, and the pet shows it.
5. **Characters are content, not code.** A character or a mod is a folder of
   files anyone can draw, share and load without touching the app.
6. **Small and dependable.** The timer and reminders keep working through
   sleep, lock and restart; a broken mod or character never takes the pet down.

## What it is not

Not a project manager, not a calendar, not a chat client, and not a game you
have to tend. The pet never dies, never guilts you, and never needs feeding.
