# Getting help

- **How do I...?** Look in the [README](README.md) first, then ask in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/LeeSwallow/petnudge/discussions), Q&A.
- **It does not work.** Open a [bug report](https://github.com/LeeSwallow/petnudge/issues/new?template=bug.yml)
  with the version you installed, your OS and a screenshot.
- **The pet did not do what I expected.** That is design feedback, the most
  useful kind of issue right now:
  [design feedback](https://github.com/LeeSwallow/petnudge/issues/new?template=design_feedback.yml).
- **A security problem.** Privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says.

petnudge has one maintainer who answers within a week, usually sooner.
There is no paid support.
